Working screens.

Presence, not surveillance. Rows show Home, Away, or Hidden — never coordinates — until you Look. The map is a secondary link, not the first screen.

02

Look confirm

Explore ↗

Looking is never silent. The sheet says who gets notified before anything is revealed. Confirm, or cancel with nothing shared.

03

Map

Explore ↗

Places before coordinates. Tap nine city pins or the name chips. This scenario assumes explicit permissions and confirmed looks. See the post-look view ↗

04

Sharing, on your terms

Explore ↗

A separate space for your permissions. Until they look, Always, or For a while. Change a mode, set a timer, or stop—directly in the row.

05

Invite

Explore ↗

Connection is not consent. Prepare an invitation or copy a demo link. Accepting an invite never silently grants location access.

06

You, in control

Explore ↗

Control, plainly. Presence is Home, Away, or Hidden. Look receipts can’t be turned off. Plus adds capacity and convenience; privacy basics stay free.

AN EDITORIAL NOTE

Less noise.
Clear rules.

Most location apps open on a map. Trust opens on people. Sealed is normal. Look notifies them first — shown before you confirm.

Paper, ink, red #E10600. Serif for Trust; system sans for controls. Maps stay secondary to Home / Away / Hidden and place lines.
