Kill the sealed map.

With ~20 contacts in trust/sealed mode, a real map is a broken promise: the UI language of “where is everyone?” with zero permitted answers. Category defaults to live pins. Trust should not.

Recommended default

Roster First

Presence is the sealed product. Home / Away / Lookable live in a masthead roll. Map appears only after Look — as a temporary reveal surface, not wallpaper. Scales to twenty people. Zero geo honesty risk.

  • A sealed map still reads as surveillance. Empty geography trains Life360 habits before Look ever happens.
  • At twenty people, lock chips on a map are noise that implies positions they do not have.
  • Home/Away without coords is already a richer sealed signal than fake latitude. Lead with that.
  • Kill “funny maps that look real.” Trust products cannot afford “is this their street?” — even as a joke.
  • A decorative globe is fine as atmosphere; it must never look like a locator. Prefer abstract wire, not Earth imagery.
  • After Look, the map can arrive as theater. Sealed Home and Looked Home should feel different on purpose.
