# App Store screenshots

The approved 25 October set is in [`2026-10-25/`](2026-10-25/README.md); it is uploaded and verified in the existing App Store Connect groups. The 2 November set is being prepared in [`2026-11-02/`](2026-11-02/README.md) from the current Trust source, with six panels ordered People, Sharing, Look, Activity, Always, Add. People and Look are captured through native UI-test interactions; Sharing, Activity, View, and Add use existing DEBUG routes. Captures use fictional fixture data only and do not prove production behavior or real account/location data. Do not replace or upload the new set until review is complete.

`capture-app-store-screenshots.sh` builds `Trust.RealServices` for testing once, then runs `capture-native-screenshots.py`. The driver selects the newest supported simulator runtime, creates and removes only simulators it owns, saves raw image hashes and test diagnostics, and does not upload anything. It does not grant the app its own location permission or open the old circle route before capture; the People screen is reached through the native Share → People flow. Duo captures require the explicitly supplied pinned hinge helper. Native capture orientation is portrait for classic iPhones, iPad, and Duo outer, and landscapeLeft for Duo inner to match its wide display. The optional phone and real-catalog paywall captures are separate from the six-panel store sequence. The builder requires all six raw captures at exact dimensions before writing panels. It composes each panel from its native raw capture and does not resize finished artwork. See [review readiness](../REVIEW-READINESS.md) for submission checks.
