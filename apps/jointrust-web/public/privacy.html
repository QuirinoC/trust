Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026. Trust is made and operated by Collapse Technologies. This policy covers the Trust iOS app and jointrust.app. Contact hello@collapsetechnologies.com.

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple: the identifier and display name Apple provides. If Apple provides no name, its email claim may be used to form a display name. Trust does not store an email address as a separate profile field.
  • The unique handle you choose. People who have completed account verification can enter that exact handle to find your Trust account and send a connection request. If you turn on “Help people find me,” someone with your complete phone number can also search for your handle. Search results do not show your Apple name, phone number, presence, location, or sharing settings. A profile picture may appear only when your discovery choice or an existing connection allows it.
  • A profile icon you select, or a photo you choose from your camera or photo library. When you save a photo, the app crops and converts it to JPEG; the API processes it again and removes embedded image metadata, including GPS and EXIF metadata, before storing it.
  • A phone number you provide to verify account ownership. The verified number stays on your account. Phone discovery is off by default, and you can turn it on or off in You. When it is on, an exact search can match the complete verified number; Trust does not show the number in search results or connection requests. If a search finds no eligible match, you can choose Invite to Trust to open native sharing; Trust does not send an invitation text or include your searched number in the invite. The app does not upload your address book. Trust does not send SMS or push notifications for connection requests.
  • Connection requests you send or receive, including their status and timing. A request expires after seven days. Accepted, declined, cancelled, and expired request metadata is kept for 30 days.
  • Precise location while you are sharing, presence such as last active time and battery, your Home or Away status, and the share mode you set for each person: Sealed, Always, Pause, or Off. Home or Away is shown to a connected person only when you separately enable Home/Away sharing with them and your share mode for them allows location (Sealed or Always). Off, Pause, and Hidden keep that status out of their view.
  • Look receipts: who looked, when, and the history window. A receipt does not contain coordinates.
  • An app installation ID and an Apple push token. The service attempts to send a receipt notification when someone looks; delivery can be delayed or may not occur.
  • Plus subscription status from Apple. We receive an entitlement, never your card number.
  • An Apple App Transaction identifier is transformed into a one-way hash and linked with its Apple environment to associate the app transaction with your Trust account. If Apple sends a consent-withdrawal notice, Trust also records Apple's notification identifier and signed and received times. Trust does not receive or store the person's age range.
  • If the app places a signed-in account on an age-related privacy hold, the API stores the account association, time, policy version, and a fixed reason that the app reported a restriction. It does not receive or store the person's age range.

How looking works

Sealed coordinates stay hidden until you confirm a look, or until that person sets Always for you. A confirmed look returns one snapshot of the latest location point Trust has stored at that time; the point may be older than the look and includes its recorded time. The service attempts a push notification about the look, but notification delivery is not guaranteed. Always allows ongoing location sharing and history while it remains enabled. History is available only in Always; the viewer's account determines whether its window is 24 hours or 30 days.

How long we keep it

  • Location: GPS points are retained for up to 30 days while stored. A viewer can read history only while you share Always with them: up to 24 hours for a Free viewer or 30 days for a Plus viewer. If you have no one left you share with, your location is cleared.
  • Profile photo: only your current photo is stored. Replacing it with another photo or a profile icon, or removing it, deletes the previous photo. It is removed when you delete your account.
  • View log: the app shows the most recent 30 days on Free and up to one year with Plus. Look-event records are retained on the service until you delete your account; events outside the app's View log window are not shown in that log.
  • SMS consent record: we keep the phone number, disclosure version and Send/Resend action, and time for each verification-text request with your account. We remove these records when you delete your account.
  • Handle, share modes, push tokens, and subscription records stay while your account exists. Pending connection requests expire after seven days; terminal request metadata is kept for 30 days.
  • Account-linked App Transaction hashes and age-related privacy holds are removed when you delete your account. Apple consent-withdrawal records retain a one-way transaction hash, Apple environment, notification identifier, and signed and received times after account deletion so the withdrawn transaction cannot be linked to a new account. These revocation records do not contain your Trust account identifier or age range and are not removed by account deletion.

Profile pictures

Your profile icon or photo is visible to you and to people with an active Trust connection. If you turn on discovery, a small picture preview may also appear in an exact handle or complete-phone search result. Turning discovery off prevents future phone matches and unconnected picture previews. Search results do not expose a public photo URL. You can change your icon or photo, or remove it, from your profile in the app.

Text messages

By tapping Send code, you agree to receive Trust verification texts at the number you entered. Standard message and data rates may apply. The service limits verification messages to at most 8 per account per day, with additional resend cooldowns and service-wide limits. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message delivery may be delayed or fail. The screen is at https://jointrust.app/sms.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Twilio transmits the verification text we ask it to send and does not use your number for its own marketing.

What we do not do

We do not sell location or any other personal data. There are no ads. Data is processed by Apple (Sign in with Apple, subscriptions, and push) and by the hosting providers that run the service. That processing does not include selling or providing your mobile phone number or SMS consent to anyone for marketing or promotional purposes.

Where it runs

The Trust API and its database run in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.

Delete your account

In the Trust iOS app, open You and choose Delete account. That removes your identity, handle, profile photo, location, people and connection requests, share settings, looks, SMS consent records, push tokens, Plus records, App Transaction account link, and age-related privacy hold from this service. Apple consent-withdrawal records are retained to prevent the withdrawn transaction from being linked to a new account, as described above. Contact us if you need help.

Age assurance

Age-related requirements vary by region. On supported iOS versions, the app requests an age range from Apple only when Apple's signals indicate a relevant regional requirement. The app evaluates the range on the device; it is not sent to or stored by Trust. Trust does not ask for a birth date or apply a global age gate.

If the app applies an age-related restriction to an existing signed-in account, it asks the Trust API to place that account on a privacy hold. The API receives no age range and cannot independently verify the app's report. The hold blocks normal account access and location sharing. Trust retains the account association, hold time, policy version, and fixed report reason until the account is deleted. The service also uses a one-way hash of Apple's App Transaction identifier and Apple environment to associate an app transaction with an account. If Apple reports withdrawn consent, Trust retains a hashed revocation record and Apple's notification identifier and timestamps after account deletion to prevent the transaction from being linked again. Trust does not store the raw App Transaction identifier or the person's age range. Trust has not designed parent-managed accounts for children under 13; this statement does not resolve Trust's legal audience classification or launch-market obligations.
