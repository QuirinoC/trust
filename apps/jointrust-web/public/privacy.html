Privacy

Last updated 24 September 2026. Trust is made and operated by Collapse Technologies. This policy covers the Trust iOS app and jointrust.app. Contact hello@collapsetechnologies.com.

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple: the identifier and display name Apple provides. If Apple provides no name, its email claim may be used to form a display name. Trust does not store an email address as a separate profile field.
  • The handle you choose.
  • A phone number you type to request account verification or create an invite. A verified phone number is stored on your account. Phone numbers entered for invites are used to create an ordinary invite and are not used to disclose whether the recipient has a Trust account.
  • Precise location while you are sharing, presence such as last active time and battery, and the share mode you set for each person: Sealed, Always, Pause, or Off.
  • Look receipts: who looked, when, and the history window. A receipt does not contain coordinates.
  • An app installation ID and an Apple push token. The service attempts to send a receipt notification when someone looks; delivery can be delayed or may not occur.
  • Plus subscription status from Apple. We receive an entitlement, never your card number.

How looking works

Sealed coordinates stay hidden until you confirm a look, or until that person sets Always for you. A confirmed look returns one current-location snapshot and records a receipt. The service attempts a push notification about the look, but notification delivery is not guaranteed. Always allows live location and history while it remains enabled. History is available only in Always; the viewer's account determines whether its window is 24 hours or 30 days.

How long we keep it

  • Location: GPS points are retained for up to 30 days while stored. A viewer can read history only while you share Always with them: up to 24 hours for a Free viewer or 30 days for a Plus viewer. If you have no one left you share with, your location is cleared.
  • Look log: 30 days on Free, one year with Plus. Look events stay on our servers until you delete your account.
  • Handle, share modes, push tokens, and subscription records stay while your account exists.

Text messages

Trust can text a verification code after you turn on SMS consent and request the code. The screen starts with consent off. The service limits verification messages to at most 8 per account per day, with additional resend cooldowns and service-wide limits. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Message delivery may be delayed or fail. The screen is at https://jointrust.app/sms.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Twilio transmits the verification text we ask it to send and does not use your number for its own marketing.

What we do not do

We do not sell location or any other personal data. There are no ads. Data is processed by Apple (Sign in with Apple, subscriptions, and push) and by the hosting providers that run the service. That processing does not include selling or providing your mobile phone number or SMS consent to anyone for marketing or promotional purposes.

Where it runs

The Trust API and its database run in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.

Delete your account

In the Trust iOS app, open You and choose Delete account. That removes your identity, handle, location, the people you added, share settings, looks, push tokens, and Plus records on this service. Contact us if you need help.

Age

Trust can be used by a household, including family. It is not a children’s app and is not directed at children under 13.
