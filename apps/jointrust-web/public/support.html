Support

Email hello@collapsetechnologies.com. Tell us your handle. Trust uses Sign in with Apple, so there is no password.

Getting started

Sign in with Apple, choose a handle, then add someone you choose. Sharing is off until you turn it on. The account needs a verification text. For looks to work while the app is closed, allow location Always in iOS Settings.

Looks and receipts

When someone looks at you, you get a push saying who looked. If receipts are not arriving, allow notifications for Trust and stay signed in on that phone.

Texts

The account needs a verification text. The box starts empty. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. The screen is at https://jointrust.app/sms.

Plus billing

Plus is $7.99 a month or $69.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, billed by Apple, on your own account. Manage, cancel, or request a refund in iOS Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. If you paid and Plus is not unlocked, open Settings in the app and restore purchases.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings, Delete account. That removes your identity, handle, location, looks, share settings, push tokens, and the people you added. You can also email us and we will delete it.
