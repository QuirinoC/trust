Support

Email hello@collapsetechnologies.com. Tell us your handle. Trust uses Sign in with Apple, so there is no password.

Getting started

Sign in with Apple, choose a handle, then add someone you choose. Adding someone does not turn on location sharing. Choose a mode for each person: Sealed sharing asks you to confirm each look, while Always allows live location. The account needs a verification text. For looks to work while the app is closed, allow location Always in iOS Settings.

Profile picture

In You, open your profile picture to choose a built-in icon, take a photo, or choose one from your photo library. A photo is saved when you tap Save and is visible to people with an active Trust connection. You can change or remove it from the same screen.

Looks and receipts

When someone looks at you, Trust requests a push notification saying who looked. Push delivery is best-effort and is not guaranteed. Activity records view receipts. If notifications are not appearing, allow notifications for Trust and check that you are signed in on that phone.

Texts

The account needs a verification text. The box starts empty. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. The screen is at https://jointrust.app/sms.

Plus billing

Plus is $7.99 a month or $69.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, billed by Apple, on your own account. Manage, cancel, or request a refund in iOS Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. If you paid and Plus is not unlocked, open You, tap See Plus, then Restore Subscription.

Delete your account

In the app: You, Delete account. If you cannot complete deletion in the app, email hello@collapsetechnologies.com for help.
