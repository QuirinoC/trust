Support

Email hello@collapsetechnologies.com. Tell us your handle. Trust uses Sign in with Apple, so there is no password.

Getting started

Sign in with Apple, choose a handle, then add someone you choose. Accepting a connection leaves sharing Off in both directions. Choose what you share with each person: Sealed returns one snapshot after a Look, while Always allows live location. Phone verification is required. For Looks to work while the app is closed, allow location Always in iOS Settings.

Profile picture

In You, open your profile picture to choose a built-in icon, take a photo, or choose one from your photo library. A photo is saved when you tap Save and is visible to people with an active Trust connection. You can change or remove it from the same screen.

Looks and receipts

When someone confirms a Sealed Look, Trust requests a push notification saying who looked. Always views do not generate push notifications. Delivery is best-effort and is not guaranteed. Activity records view receipts. If Sealed Look notifications are not appearing, allow notifications for Trust and check that you are signed in on that phone.

Texts

Phone verification is required. A code is sent only after you enter a number and tap Send code. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Read the text consent details at https://jointrust.app/sms.

Plus billing

Plus is an optional subscription billed by Apple on your own account. Apple shows the current price, billing period, and any introductory offer before purchase. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions; request refunds through Apple. If you paid and Plus is not unlocked, open You, tap See Plus, then Restore Subscription.

Delete your account

In the app: You, Delete account. If you cannot complete deletion in the app, email hello@collapsetechnologies.com for help.
