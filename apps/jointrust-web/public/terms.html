How looking works

Your coordinates stay hidden until someone you added confirms a look, unless you set Always for that person. A look is one snapshot and sends a receipt. Free history is 24 hours. Plus history is 30 days. You can remove anyone at any time, and you can delete your account in Settings. Free includes up to 5 people.
