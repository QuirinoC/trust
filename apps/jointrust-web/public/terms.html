Who it is for

Trust is for people who choose to share with one another, including family. You need an account, a handle, and phone verification. You decide who you invite and the share mode for each connected person. Accepting an invite does not turn on location sharing.

Age-related requirements vary by region and iOS version. Where Apple signals a relevant regional requirement, supported iOS versions may request an age range or require an approval for a significant app update. The standard sign-up does not ask for a birth date, and Trust does not apply a blanket 13+ gate. Trust has not designed parent-managed accounts for children under 13; this statement does not resolve Trust's legal audience classification or launch-market obligations. Some required Apple flows may be unavailable on older iOS versions.

If a parent or guardian withdraws consent through Apple and Trust receives and verifies Apple's consent-withdrawal notice, Trust deletes the linked Trust account and its data and permanently blocks relinking that withdrawn app transaction. The current implementation has no consent-restoration path. Apple may also prevent the app from launching for that child.

How looking works

With Sealed sharing, your coordinates stay hidden until a connected person confirms a look. A confirmed look returns one snapshot of the latest location point Trust has stored at that time; it may be older than the look and includes its recorded time. Trust attempts a push notification for a confirmed Sealed Look; delivery can be delayed or may not occur. Always views do not generate a push notification. Always sharing allows ongoing location sharing and history. History is available only in Always, for up to 24 hours for a Free viewer or 30 days for a Plus viewer. You control each share mode, can remove a connection, and can delete your account from You in the app. Free includes up to 5 people.

Plus

Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription bought through Apple. Apple shows the price, billing period, and any introductory offer available to you before purchase. Plus is on your own account. It is not shared with the people you add. Plus adds Always, up to 20 people, live pins, 30 days of history, and a one-year look log with export. Family Sharing is off.
