# Trust iOS design

## Direction

Trust uses a premium native iOS interface with a cool porcelain canvas, white elevated surfaces, deep navy text, electric blue actions, and teal positive states. Use rounded system typography, clear hierarchy, 20–24 pt page gutters, 16–24 pt corner radii, and controls at least 44 pt tall. The palette adapts to dark mode: navy canvas, lighter navy surfaces, readable ink, and brighter blue and teal accents. Avoid serif display fonts, gradients, decorative mastheads, and oversized marketing panels.

## Screen intent

- **Sign in:** state the sharing choice clearly and make Sign in with Apple the primary action. Keep legal and support links easy to reach.
- **Handle:** explain the public handle rules, show availability feedback, and keep submission disabled for invalid or taken handles.
- **Phone:** require a separate, unchecked SMS consent choice before sending a code. Make code entry, resend, verification, and errors clear.
- **People:** keep the map and people list together. Use the shared bottom navigation for People, Sharing, Activity, and You.
- **Sharing:** show each relationship's current sharing mode and its controls without implying that adding someone starts sharing.
- **Activity:** show recorded events and receipts with their status and timing.
- **You:** collect profile, presence, Home place, Plus, account, and policy controls in one place.
- **Plus:** use StoreKit's subscription products for price and period, preserve restore and policy links, and show purchase or restore errors clearly.

## Product language

Sharing is a per-person choice. Avoid implying that Trust guarantees delivery of push notifications or SMS beyond the verification flow. Describe map, presence, and activity only as implemented; distinguish a one-time snapshot from live sharing.
