# Trust staging plan

Staging is a planned, isolated end-to-end environment for two physical iPhones. Its status and outstanding prerequisites are in [operations/staging/STATUS.md](operations/staging/STATUS.md); the [physical-device guide](PHYSICAL-DEVICE-TEST.md) is the acceptance procedure. Staging infrastructure and software delivery are complete, including the live API and web Worker, Apple app and catalog, and build 49 available in internal TestFlight; the status record distinguishes verified resources and checks from work that remains unprovisioned or unverified.

## Boundaries

- Build `TrustStaging` from the same Trust source, with bundle identifier `com.collapsetechnologies.trust.staging`. Target the staging API at `https://staging-api.jointrust.app` and staging invite links at `https://staging.jointrust.app`.
- Provision a separate Render service and PostgreSQL database, and a separate Apple app and TestFlight distribution. Configure the staging app's notification entitlement and APNs credentials for its own identifier and the APNs environment required by TestFlight. Use an independent staging database and JWT secret, and separate staging test accounts. Do not use production customer data, production database data, or production SMS credentials. Approved existing company App Store Connect, signing and team APNs credentials may be reused for the staging bundle and topic with verified protected references; check the inventory before creating duplicates.
- Only in staging, allow the two designated fictional phone numbers to be automatically verified: A `+12025550101`, B `+12025550102`. Limit any bypass to this isolated environment and these exact numbers; verify that production cannot use it. This lane does not test carrier SMS, delivery, or the production phone-verification flow.
- Use real Apple Sign In, device location permissions and location, Apple Sandbox StoreKit purchases, and APNs. Age-range prompts and mandatory App Store app-transaction registration are paused as of 29 October 2026; verify that sign-in proceeds without those screens. Purchase verification remains required. Do not substitute local entitlement fixtures, simulated pushes, or mocked location outcomes for these checks. TestFlight APNs uses the production APNs environment; observed delivery remains best effort.
- Separate Apple IDs on the two phones exercise two distinct accounts. They do not validate same-account handoff across devices.

## Provisioning and release checklist

1. Review [staging status](operations/staging/STATUS.md), current source and release records. Confirm owners, intended testers, and isolation before making changes.
2. Check `/Users/juanquirino/.agents/operations/credential-inventory.json` before creating credentials. For each credential or operational resource, record purpose, provider/account, resource or key identifier, exact scope, protected storage reference, creation and verification dates, expiry or rotation requirements, and recovery/revocation steps there. Link project-specific records; update them after changes or revocation. Keep secret values only in an appropriate secret manager, OS Keychain, or deliberately protected file; never in source, ordinary documentation, logs, screenshots, or chat. Verify protected storage and its reference before claiming backup or configuration.
3. Create and record the isolated database and service, domains and TLS, Apple app and TestFlight access, signing and notification configuration, and staging-only verification controls. Record actual identifiers and evidence in the status record; retain **NOT PROVISIONED** until verified. Do not copy production customer data, production database data or production SMS credentials into staging; approved existing company Apple credentials may be reused as described above.
4. Deploy the reviewed staging API and migrations, then verify health, isolation, routes, the exact-number restriction and production rejection. Build the same reviewed source as `TrustStaging`, verify its identifier and endpoints, and distribute through the staging TestFlight app. For the internal-only upload use `xcodebuild -exportArchive` with the repository upload destination and internal flag; generic altool upload may lose channel metadata. Record source revision, build, migration and verification evidence. Do not treat a planned endpoint or successful upload as proof of an operational environment.
5. Configure real Apple Sandbox products and tester access for both accounts. Verify purchase and restore against the staging app; never change release or entitlement flags to bypass gates.
6. Run the [physical-device procedure](PHYSICAL-DEVICE-TEST.md). Record date, build, device/iOS versions, test roles, results and limitations without secrets or customer data. Investigate failures and rerun affected cases. Clean up the test accounts and document remaining resources and revocation or recovery steps.

Existing simulator and local evidence is summarized in [FEATURE-E2E-PLAN.md](FEATURE-E2E-PLAN.md); it does not establish physical TestFlight or staging acceptance.
