## Privacy worksheet

The source [privacy manifest](../../../../apps/trust-ios/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares all 12 categories below linked to the user, used for app functionality, and not used for tracking. It declares no tracking domains. These are proposed reconciliation answers grounded in the manifest and observed source, not a substitute for checking all providers and the live label.

- **Name:** account display name and optional profile editing; identifies people in connections and notifications. Linked; app functionality; no tracking.
- **Phone number:** verified account ownership, optional exact-number discovery and verification texts. Consent and verification-operation metadata are retained with the account. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. The address book is not uploaded. See [phone service](../../../../apps/trust-api/Application/PhoneVerificationService.cs) and [policy](../../../../apps/jointrust-web/public/privacy.html).
- **Precise location:** location points, timestamps and per-person sharing support snapshots, Always and history under authorized modes. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. A snapshot is the latest available stored point and can be old; consent precedes sharing. Policy states points retained up to 30 days and cleared when no active sharing remains.
- **Coarse location:** when a person chooses reduced-accuracy location, approximate shared location points can be stored and associated with their account for authorized sharing. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. Apple’s [data definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) include approximate location services. Separately, the optional phone country suggestion uses a network-derived approximate country and does not request GPS permission; the policy says the application does not log or store the country or IP for that lookup.
- **Product interaction:** Look/View and activity records, share choices and receipts support sharing and history. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. The policy distinguishes the display window from retained Look-event records.
- **Contacts:** chosen Trust account-to-account relationships and connection state, not the device address book. Linked; app functionality; no tracking.
- **User ID:** Apple identity/account association and Trust account identifier support sign-in, account state and recovery. Linked; app functionality; no tracking.
- **Device ID:** installation ID and push registration/token support device notifications and troubleshooting. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. Registration storage is in [PostgresPushDeviceStore.cs](../../../../apps/trust-api/Infrastructure/Notifications/PostgresPushDeviceStore.cs); APNs delivery is best effort.
- **Purchase history:** Apple transaction and product identifiers, subscription status and recovery information support entitlement verification and restoration. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. See [StoreKit authority](../../../../apps/trust-api/Infrastructure/StoreKit/StoreKitSubscriptionAuthority.cs).
- **Photos or videos:** an optionally selected or captured profile image is staged until Save; the policy says images are converted and metadata removed before storage, with visibility governed by connection/discovery. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. See [profile picker](../../../../apps/trust-ios/Sources/TrustApp/ProfileAvatarPicker.swift).
- **Other diagnostic data:** operational diagnostics can include account and installation IDs and APNs errors. Linked; app functionality; no tracking. Verify actual production logging and provider settings before final answers.
- **Other data types:** recovery requests, verification operation/consent records, and applicable historic privacy-hold and consent-withdrawal records support account operation and recovery. Linked; app functionality; no tracking per manifest; assess each field and retention exception against Apple’s current category definitions. See [recovery actions](../../../../apps/trust-ios/Sources/TrustApp/RecoverySupportActions.swift) and [privacy policy](../../../../apps/jointrust-web/public/privacy.html).

The manifest’s UserDefaults accessed-API reason is `CA92.1`; reconcile the manifest and all included dependencies separately from the App Privacy questionnaire. The policy describes retained consent-withdrawal records surviving account deletion without a Trust account identifier; avoid a blanket statement that every record is deleted.

## Reviewer walkthrough draft

**Prerequisites, not yet established:** a usable production review path needs genuine Apple sign-in, a phone number able to receive a real verification text, and a second verified Trust account/person for connected sharing. Provide the reviewer with approved access details through App Store Connect’s secure review fields once available; do not invent credentials, use staging reserved numbers, or enable a development/review bypass. A paid walkthrough additionally needs a genuine Apple Sandbox purchase or another verified, reviewable entitlement path. Positive connected and paid reviewer access, genuine purchase, server entitlement and restore have not been established.

**Proposed review notes, to complete only after prerequisites are verified:**

1. Install the normal Trust build. Sign in with Apple, enter the supplied usable phone number, request and enter the verification code, and continue to People. Trust automatically assigns a handle. Existing accounts with verified phones may proceed without another text.
2. Open Sharing → Add someone. Search the supplied second account’s exact handle or its opted-in complete phone number. Send a request and accept it on the second account. Both directions start Off.
3. On the sharing account, choose Sealed for the connected person and confirm the named recipient. Use My Location to grant the requested location permission and provide a location. On the recipient account, open that person and request a Look. The returned snapshot is the latest available point, and Activity records the interaction. A receipt push is attempted, not guaranteed.
4. To review Always, complete the actual Apple Sandbox subscription flow on the **sharing** account and confirm entitlement; select Always and confirm sharing. The connected recipient may view the authorized point without purchasing Plus. Verify the displayed location age and the applicable history window. Use the app’s restore action only with the relevant Apple account and an existing purchase.
5. In Sharing, select Off or Remove to stop the relevant access; in You, review discovery, profile, notifications, subscription management and account deletion. Deletion is a confirmed action and does not cancel Apple billing.

Do not present this draft as an established end-to-end review path until its credentials, two-account connection, location, paid entitlement and restore are exercised against the intended normal environment. Record actual result and any constraints without including credentials, SMS codes or personal locations in this file.
